
BlueScape Pools Company Information

About Us
BlueScape Pools is a swimming pool design and contruction company serving Sydney and Greater Areas of Sydney. We help customers explore pool designs, visualize their backyards and arrange site assessments. 


Pool Options

Coastal - A modern rectangular fibreglass pool for compact backyards, featuring bench seating for relaxation.

Family - A rectangular fibreglass pool with broad entry steps, a seating ledge and a gradual depth transition, designed for family recreation.

Retreat - A compact fibreglass plunge pool designed for cooling off and relaxation in smaller outdoor spaces.

Dimensions, depths, colours and availability must be confirmed with our team. A site assessment is required to determine which options suit a property.

Pricing
Each project requires an individual quotation. Pricing depends on the pool design, site conditions, excavation, access and selected extras.

The chatbot cannot provide a final price, guarantee that a budget is sufficient or offer discounts.


Installation Process

1. Discuss the customer's requirements and preferred design.

2. Assess the site, access and project requirements.

3. Prepare the proposal and identify necessary approvals.

4. Carry out excavation and pool installation.

5. Complete the contracted equipment and surrounding works.

6. Provide a handover explaining pool operation and maintenance.

Installation dates and project duration require confirmation from our team.


Optional Features
Customers can discuss:

Availability, compatibility and additional costs require confirmation. These features are not automatically included in a quotation.


Backyard Design Mockups
Customers can submit a backyard photo and describe their preferred pool and surroundings.

Our design service produces visual mockups to help customers explore ideas. These images do not confirm exact dimensions, construction feasibility or approval.


Maintenance
Pools require regular cleaning, water testing and equipment care. Customers should follow their supplied pool and equipment instructions.

Chemical dosing, equipment faults, leaks and structural concerns should be referred to qualified staff.

Warranty
Warranty coverage depends on the pool, equipment and installation contract. Contact our team for the applicable written terms.

The chatbot must not invent warranty periods or guarantee that a particular issue is covered.
